Chapter One · 下層

Ground
Rain

Street level is the only place in Rokujō where the sky is a rumour. Sixty metres of overbuild sit on top of it, so the weather arrives second-hand: condensation off the transit deck, runoff from the towers, a fine chemical drizzle that never fully stops. The neon does not illuminate the street. It stains it.

Light Every sign is a real emitter. Nothing here is painted on — the puddles are lit by the signs above them, and only by those.
Sound Rain on corrugate, a compressor two doors down, and the low sixty-hertz hum of a district that never powers off.
Rule Look up and you lose your place in the queue.

The city stopped being built and started being stacked.

Field note — Rokujō, 23:47

Chapter Two · 積層

The
Stack

Above the shopfronts the district turns vertical and impersonal. Transit rails thread between cores at four levels. Freight drones hold station in the gaps. From here the towers stop reading as buildings and start reading as circuitry seen edge-on — lit rows, dark rows, a fault line of dead floors where a landlord stopped paying.

Geometry Cores, setbacks, service masts and antenna forests are grown from a seeded noise field — never the same block twice.
Traffic Rail light is a moving emitter, not a texture. It arrives, it passes, it takes its reflection with it.
Rule Altitude is rent. Everyone above you is paying for your sky.

Chapter Three · 電脳市

Wired
Market

The market is the one level with a face. A hundred metres of projection surface, half of it broken, all of it selling. The big holograph on the east core has been mid-sentence for eleven years — the loop survived the company that bought it. Nobody turns it off, because nobody remembers who could.

Holography Rendered as additive scan planes with per-line jitter, so the figure breathes and tears the way real volumetric projection does in rain.
Type Every sign glyph is drawn to an off-screen canvas at load and uploaded once. No font files leave the machine.
Rule If it is free to look at, you are the inventory.

Chapter Four · 上昇

Above
The Line

At six hundred metres the rain stops being weather and becomes a floor. The smog line is flat, lit from underneath by everything you just walked through, and the noise finally drops out. What is left is the orbital relay coming up in the east, a red disc large enough to be architecture, and the first silence in four kilometres.

Palette Cyan and magenta stay below the cloud deck. Up here the scene resolves to near-black, bone and vermilion — the city as a glow, not a place.
Camera One continuous path, five composed shots. Never a cut.

Colophon · 切れません

Built From
Nothing

  1. No image is ever loaded. Every texture in this page is drawn to a canvas by seeded noise at load time, then uploaded once to the GPU — the one picture in the repository is the social card, which the page itself never fetches.
  2. No build step, no framework, no package manager. One HTML file and a local copy of three.js.
  3. No network calls at runtime. No fonts, no analytics, no trackers. Open it offline and it behaves identically.
  4. One camera path. Scroll is the only input, and every shot is a position on that path rather than a scene swap.
  5. Reduced motion is a first-class route, not a fallback — the full text and every plate survive it.
